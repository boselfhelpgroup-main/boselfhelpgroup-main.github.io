BO Welfare GroupMember Portal

Your welfare account, in one secure place

The member portal of B.O Welfare Self Help Group (BOSHG) brings together Bank Officers and Clerical Assistants who support one another as one family.

Open the member portal

What members can do

  • View your statementYour contributions and account history.
  • Check your beneficiariesSee who is declared on your record.
  • Update your detailsKeep your phone number and email current.
  • Download reportsReports the Welfare shares with all members.
  • Find your membership numberLook it up with your Bank Staff Number.
  • Apply to joinNew members can apply online.

The BOSHG Android app

The app gives members the same portal on their phone. The first time it opens, the member signs in with their Google account so the Welfare can confirm the phone belongs to them. After that they sign in with their Membership Number and PIN as usual.
