Privacy Policy

BOSHG Member Portal and Android app · Last updated 10 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information B.O Welfare Self Help Group ("the Welfare", "we") keeps in the BOSHG Member Portal and the BOSHG Android app, why we keep it, and your rights. It applies to members, applicants and anyone who uses the portal or the app.

1. Information we keep

  • Membership details: your name, membership number, Bank staff number, national ID number, branch and region, mobile number and email address.
  • Beneficiaries: the family members you declare (names and phone numbers) so that benefits can be paid.
  • Contributions and claims: your monthly payments, your contribution totals and any claims or benefits paid.
  • Applications: when you apply to join, the details on the form and the proof of payment you upload.
  • Your PIN: stored only in scrambled (hashed) form. Nobody at the Welfare can read it.
  • Sign-in and security records: the time of sign-ins and important actions, the device type, browser and operating system, and the network address your browser reports.
  • Android app only: when you choose "Sign in with Google", Google tells us your Google account email address and a Google account ID. We use them only to confirm that the phone belongs to you. The app keeps a sign-in key on your phone so you are not asked again for 90 days.

2. Why we use it

  • To run your membership, record your contributions and pay benefits under the Constitution.
  • To contact you about your membership, payments, claims and PIN resets.
  • To keep accounts secure: to stop people guessing PINs and to investigate misuse.
  • To keep the records the Constitution requires and to support the annual audit.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone outside the Welfare except where the law requires it.

3. Who can see it

  • You can see and update your own details in the portal.
  • Office bearers see only what their role needs. For example, the Treasurer sees payments and claims, and the Chairperson sees what needs their approval. Regional Representatives see limited details of members in their region and never your beneficiaries' names.
  • Sign-in and security records are seen only by the system administrator.

4. Where it is kept and for how long

  • The portal runs on Google services (Google Apps Script, Google Sheets and Google Drive), in the Welfare's own Google account.
  • Membership, contribution and claim records are kept for as long as you are a member and afterwards as long as the Constitution, the audit or the law requires.
  • Sign-in and security records are deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • The app's sign-in key expires after 90 days. Uninstalling the app removes it from your phone.

5. Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 you may ask to see the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, and object to how it is used. You can update most of your details yourself in the portal. For anything else, contact the Secretary or email us at the address below.

6. Children

The portal and the app are for members and applicants, who are adult Bank employees. Children's names are kept only when a member declares them as beneficiaries.

7. Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above. Important changes will also be shared with members.

8. Contact
